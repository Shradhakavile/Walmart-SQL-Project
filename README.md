**Problem Statement**
Retail businesses rely on sales data to understand customer behavior, product performance, and branch-level profitability.
This project analyzes Walmart sales data using SQL to uncover revenue drivers, customer purchasing patterns, and product-line trends, enabling better marketing, pricing, and inventory decisions.

**Dataset Overview**
- Walmart sales dataset containing information on branch, city, customer type, gender, product line, unit price, quantity, total sales, gross income, ratings, payment method, date, and time
- Format: CSV
- Used for structured business analysis and insight generation

**Project Objectives**
- Evaluate sales performance across branches and cities
- Identify top-performing product lines
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior by gender
- Examine trends in gross income and customer ratings
- Generate actionable business insights using SQL

**SQL Techniques Used**
- Data filtering and aggregation (SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, ORDER BY)
- Aggregate functions (SUM, COUNT, AVG, ROUND)
- Aliasing for readability
- Subqueries for layered analysis
- Conditional filtering for business-driven insights

**Key Business Insights**
- Certain product lines consistently outperform others in terms of total units sold.
- Female customers contribute higher sales in multiple high-performing product categories.
- Branch-wise analysis revealed uneven revenue distribution across locations.
- Identified the city with the highest average customer rating, indicating stronger customer satisfaction.
- Transactions with gross income below ₹50 highlight low-margin sales requiring pricing or cost optimization.

**Outputs & Documentation**
- Structured SQL queries addressing each business question
- Query outputs exported and documented
- Screenshots of queries and results included in the Results/ folder

**Tools Used**
- MySQL Workbench
- SQL
- CSV Dataset
- GitHub (project documentation)

**Conclusion**
This project demonstrates the ability to use SQL for retail sales analysis, translating transactional data into meaningful insights.
It showcases skills in data exploration, business analysis, and insight communication, relevant for Data Analyst and Business Analyst roles.
